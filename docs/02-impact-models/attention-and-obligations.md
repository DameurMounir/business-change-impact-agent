# Attention tiers and obligations

Attention tiers are transparent business rules. They are not probability, model confidence, approval or risk acceptance. Entity type, domain, directness, evidence gaps and multi-change collisions determine the tier and produce visible reason codes.

Obligations are proposals for human review. They describe what should be confirmed or evidenced before implementation planning. They do not allocate staff, approve spending, select vendors, execute change or decide go-live.
