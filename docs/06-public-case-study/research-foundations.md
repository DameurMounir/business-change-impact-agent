# Research and engineering foundations

The implementation uses authoritative sources as design references rather than as claims that the synthetic case is certified:

- [IIBA, Business Analysis Standard](https://www.iiba.org/professional-development/knowledge-centre/business-analysis-standard/) — business-analysis practices and traceability context.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — transparent governance, measurement and human oversight principles.
- [JSON Schema Draft 2020-12](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12) — public contract vocabulary.
- [SQLite transaction documentation](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_transaction.html) — local transactional review persistence.
- [OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/) — prompt-injection and output-handling threat awareness.
- [Python packaging specifications](https://packaging.python.org/en/latest/specifications/) — wheel and source-distribution packaging boundaries.

These references support design choices. They do not constitute external validation of the repository’s outcomes or a production compliance claim.
