# Limitations

- The evidence, organisation and impacts are fictional.
- The deterministic rule baseline is intentionally narrow and does not discover relationships absent from the graph.
- Conditional impacts remain open until their stated evidence gaps are closed.
- The human reviewer remains responsible for confirmation, revision or rejection.
- The repository does not execute change, allocate resources, approve risk or decide go-live.
- Live-model evaluation is not run in v0.1.0.
